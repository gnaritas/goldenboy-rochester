The room stays golden.

Music isn’t decoration here — it’s half the concept. Goldenboy is a modern take on a 1970s vibe: a warm, social room with DJs scheduled to spin records most nights of the week.

Most nights, on records

DJs are scheduled to spin records most nights of the week — a room built for talking in, dancing near, and staying late. The bar runs to midnight, so the night ends when you decide it does.

The 1970s, modernized

The look is what the crew calls a modern take on a retro 1970s vibe — the warmth of the era without the museum bit. Manager Daniel Rehor’s pitch for the room, quoted by WROC: “a social, bar-forward experience with a really incredible food program.” The playlist is what that sounds like at nine p.m.

What’s on tonight?

Opening-week schedules move fast — DJ nights included. The shop’s Facebook page is where the week’s nights get posted first.
