The Bar

The night starts
at the bar.

Goldenboy flips the Park Avenue script. Manager Daniel Rehor put it plainly to WROC: most spots on the avenue are restaurant-forward with great bar programs — Goldenboy is built the other way around, a social bar-forward room with an incredible food program behind it.
