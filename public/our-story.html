Our Story

Three restaurant people.
One Park Avenue room.

Goldenboy is the new project of Eric Rozestraten, Jay Speranza and Ron Billitier — named in WROC’s opening coverage — who took the former Starbucks at 650 Park Avenue, gave it a modern take on a 1970s vibe, and staffed the kitchen and bar with talent brought over from Shell Restaurant downtown.

The crew

Rozestraten · Speranza · Billitier

The partners behind Goldenboy are Eric Rozestraten, Jay Speranza and Ron Billitier, per WROC’s November 2026 coverage. Rozestraten and Speranza also run Cotoletta ROC, and the trio has a second Park Avenue project, Rosso, on the way.

The space

The old Starbucks on Park Avenue

Crews spent the fall transforming the former Starbucks storefront at 650 Park Avenue into a bar-forward restaurant — a warm, retro-modern room where a national chain used to pour coffee.

The hire

A chef and a bartender from Shell

The food and drink programs came ready-made: the owners brought both the chef and the bartender over from Shell Restaurant downtown, then let them build Goldenboy’s kitchen and bar list.

Opening

Soft opening Thanksgiving weekend

The doors go open over Thanksgiving weekend 2026, with a grand opening to follow the week after. Kitchen Tuesday–Sunday, 4–10 pm. Bar until midnight.
