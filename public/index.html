The story so far

The old Starbucks became the new night out

Owners Eric Rozestraten, Jay Speranza and Ron Billitier took the former Starbucks at 650 Park Avenue and gave it a modern take on a 1970s vibe — then brought a chef and a bartender over from Shell Restaurant downtown. The soft opening runs over Thanksgiving weekend; the grand opening follows.
