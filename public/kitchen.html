The Kitchen

Delicious, comforting,
easy to wrap your head around.

That’s the manager’s own description, and the kitchen is no afterthought: a chef the owners poached from Shell Restaurant downtown, cooking real plates for a bar crowd, Tuesday through Sunday from four until ten.

Plan your visit Back to the bar

1The plates the press knows

What to come for, straight from manager Daniel Rehor’s preview to WROC: “The burger is going to be great. Come for the French dip. We’ll have a delicious lobster mac and cheese.” Nothing fussy — food built to be shared, ordered late, finished before the bar goes to midnight.

2A chef from downtown

The chef and the bartender both came over from Shell Restaurant, the downtown Rochester spot the owners named as their source of talent. That’s the pedigree behind Goldenboy’s kitchen and bar — hired together, from one of the city’s best-regarded rooms.

Kitchen 4–10, bar to midnight

Dinner runs Tuesday through Sunday, four until ten — and the bar keeps pouring until midnight after the last plate. Monday visits come early: the kitchen is for the rest of the week.
